What's new in v1.3.8

Mandarin v1.3.8, since v1.3.7, with Mandarin OS 0.1.16 and Mandarin Bridge v0.3.21. An even album page with a + to add Qobuz and Tidal albums, one Random albums set, and the page fixed on older iPads.

  • Changed: an album's page is even: its title and artists centred, and the heart, Play Now, Queue and ⋯ on one row.
  • Changed: a Qobuz or Tidal album not in your favourites there has a + in the heart's place to add it; the heart takes its place once added.
  • Fixed: Random albums is one set: Home's row matches the Random albums screen, new only on Refresh, after 24 hours, or every ten minutes in Android Auto.
  • Fixed: the web page works on older iPads (iOS 16): the menu, Search, Artists, the mini player, Random Album and album pages.
  • Fixed: an album added to your Qobuz or Tidal favourites says so under the artist at once.
Updating: the server from Settings → Updates, the Android app beside it, each Mandarin Bridge from its own row. Mandarin OS updates itself from its system page; 0.1.3 and earlier reinstall 0.1.16 once (how). Every change is listed in the changelog.

Before: v1.3.6

Mandarin v1.3.6, since v1.3.5, with Mandarin OS 0.1.15 and Mandarin Bridge v0.3.21. One library from everywhere your music is: an album you have in several places shows once, with its versions; an artist's page shows their whole discography from your default service; and iBroadcast albums get their folders' covers and booklets, and can be merged or deleted.

One album, wherever it is

An album you have in several places (your files, Qobuz, Tidal, iBroadcast) shows once. Its Versions tab lists every copy with where it is from, its edition and year. The main one is the one you chose, else your default service's, else the best.

Default service and the whole discography

In Settings → Services, under the services: None, Qobuz or Tidal. An artist's Discography then lists that service's albums of theirs you don't have, under More on Qobuz (or Tidal); a tap opens one in that service's browser. An edition you have isn't listed again.

Merge albums

Select two or more albums of your own files, or all iBroadcast's, then ⋯ → Merge albums: one album of several discs, the first disc first, whichever you picked first. A merged album offers Unmerge.

Delete from iBroadcast

Select iBroadcast albums, then ⋯ → Delete from iBroadcast. You are asked twice: what it does, then Are you sure? No to either lets go of the selection; Yes deletes them from your iBroadcast account and from Mandarin at once.

iBroadcast covers and booklets

iBroadcast albums you uploaded with Mandarin show their upload folder's cover and booklets (PDFs), kept in Mandarin so they show with the drive away; Settings shows the booklets kept. Backups carry them (iBroadcast covers and booklets). An iBroadcast album with no picture shows a drawn cover.

Keys built in

Mandarin carries its own AcoustID and FanArt.tv keys: nothing to enter for matching albums by sound or for artists' photos.

  • Removed: Last.fm (scrobbling, and Settings → API Keys). Similar artists and albums come from Deezer, with no key.
  • Removed: record labels: the Labels screen and its logos, Label of the week on Home, and labels in search. An album's label is still read, and shown in Edit album's Version.
  • Fixed: iBroadcast discs uploaded as albums of their own (The Wall (Disc 1), The Wall (Disc 2)) are one album.
  • Fixed: iBroadcast's library is read where iBroadcast moved a track to another album; an album new to the library plays on the first Play Now; signing in works while iBroadcast is slow; and an upload no longer sends files again before iBroadcast has listed them.
  • Fixed: away from home, screens opened at home follow and play in this phone's room; Now playing no longer shows the phone's own queue for a server's room after a moment offline.
  • Fixed: a cover no longer fails to draw now and then when many are asked for at once.
  • Fixed: on a new library, streamed albums brought in before the first scan finished are no longer renamed and added again.

Before: v1.3.5

Mandarin v1.3.5, since v1.3.4, with Mandarin OS 0.1.12 and Mandarin Bridge v0.3.21. Uploads to iBroadcast are easier to live with: a limit on how much of your internet upload they use, any number of drives at once, and files with long or accented names kept.

Upload bandwidth

In Settings → Services → iBroadcast → Upload bandwidth: enter your upload speed and choose how much of it uploads may use (25, 50, 75 or 100 %). The rest is left for everything else on your connection.

Upload from several drives

Upload from takes any number of your drives at once, or All drives (it was All music). Each file is sent once, and a chosen drive that isn't connected is left out.

Long and accented file names

iBroadcast now keeps files with long or accented names, such as an opera box set's tracks. A file it takes but doesn't keep is no longer sent again on every upload: it is left, and listed with iBroadcast's reason.

  • Changed: an upload's speed is shown in Mbps.
  • Fixed: Upload from → All music sent nothing where your drives aren't music folders.
  • Fixed: an iBroadcast album chosen to play while MusicBrainz is busy is matched by sound the next time you choose it.

Before: v1.3.4

Mandarin v1.3.4, since v1.3.3, with Mandarin OS 0.1.12 and Mandarin Bridge v0.3.21. Mandarin now works with iBroadcast: your iBroadcast library is in Mandarin's and plays everywhere Mandarin plays, can be kept on the phone, and is where your own library is uploaded to.

iBroadcast in Mandarin

Sign in from Settings → Services → iBroadcast with a code, as on a TV. Your iBroadcast albums join the library, on the walls, in search and in Smart Picks.

iBroadcast plays everywhere

In every room, on every Mandarin Bridge and on the phone, each track as you uploaded it, at its own rate.

Upload to iBroadcast

Upload now, or each day on a schedule, from all your music or one drive, each file as it is and nothing sent twice. Choose how many files at once, and whether it goes on while music plays at home or away. An upload under way carries on after a restart.

iBroadcast albums like your own

Kept on the phone with Download to this phone, changed in Edit album (in Mandarin only), and identified by the Album Scanner, by their sound when you choose one to play.

Export library

In the side menu: your library as a CSV file for Mandarin Migration.

Qobuz upsampled

Qobuz tracks are upsampled, or made DSD, as your own files are, by each device's Sound settings.

The Library Scanner

One switch, Album Scanner, in Settings → Library Scanner: albums identified, ReplayGain measured and missing covers found, with one Progress readout and an optional Schedule. It waits only while Mandarin's own music plays in a room you use, and says which. The albums it can't place wait in Unidentified and To confirm. Features →

Identification that finds your edition

A disc set is one album, a CD rip is found by its tracks' exact lengths, and the pressing that fits your copy best is the one chosen, the other editions a tap away. Every match says how sure it is and why; To confirm has Apply and Not this, and Mandarin learns from your choices.

Matching by sound

With a free AcoustID key in Settings → Setup → API Keys, an album no search by name can place is fingerprinted on the server and matched by how it sounds. Match by sound in Edit album does one album at once.

Covers found automatically

An album with no cover of its own is given one from Apple Music, Deezer or the Cover Art Archive, only when its title, artist and tracks all agree. A cover in its folder or its files, or one you chose, is never touched.

Edit album: the version, and copies apart

Version shows the pressing's name, label, catalogue number, barcode, country, year and format, each yours to change. Two releases merged into one album come apart, by folder at the next scan or with Split… under Folders and copies. Your files are never changed.

MQA passthrough

MQA files are recognised and marked with their original rate (MQA · 24/352.8 original), and found under Focus → Format. Mandarin sends MQA untouched to a DAC set to This DAC decodes MQA in Settings → Audio Devices. Mandarin doesn't decode or unfold MQA itself: a DAC that decodes MQA, fully unfolding it, is needed. Any other room plays it as ordinary PCM. What each device is sent →

SQ-tool compares two songs

Compare two songs null-tests song A against song B: a verdict in plain words, where in the spectrum they differ, spectrograms and loudness, and the difference to download. The bit-perfect check now works with Mandarin Bridge for Linux and the Raspberry Pi too. SQ-tool →

One log

Everything Mandarin says, from the server, Mandarin OS, every Bridge, the app and the page, a line each in time order in one plain-text file, without personal data: on Mandarin OS, smb://<box address>/Data/Logs. Logs →

Clipping shown, and Headroom when upsampling

A track mastered to full scale clips between its samples when upsampled. Mandarin now counts every clipped sample and turns Now playing's line red; Headroom when upsampling (Auto) turns a track down by just what its true peak needs. Upsampling →

Keeping up, and a safety net

The signal path says how fast a track is being made (Keeping up at 4.2× real time). An Output the server can't make in time, or the device can't play, is put back to the last one that played well, the track carrying on. Signal path →

Security

A security audit of this version found fourteen things to put right; every one is fixed.

  • Who sent a request is believed from forwarding headers only from the proxies named in TRUSTED_PROXIES and the built-in Tailscale. A reverse proxy on the same machine needs TRUSTED_PROXIES=127.0.0.1. Configuration →
  • Covers from elsewhere are never fetched from the server's own machine or your network (but the Sonos speakers and hosts named in ART_HOSTS).
  • Addresses handed to speakers and players expire after 30 days, and a speaker asking without one gets only what it was handed.
  • Mandarin Bridge's port takes changes only as JSON, never from another site, and never updates backwards. A page on another port of the server's machine can't use your browser's sign-in either.
  • Scripts run only from Mandarin's own pages (a Content-Security-Policy).
  • With the sign-in off, only your own Tailscale devices count as home, not all of Tailscale's address range.
  • Releases can be signed, and the server's and the Linux Bridge's updates checked against Mandarin's release key.
  • Away from home, use Tailscale, never a port forward: the log now says when plain http arrives from outside. The workflows' actions are pinned, and a build that can be published needs the real signing key. Away from home →

Fixed

  • An album that mixes sample rates played nothing with Albums as one stream on, when its Output made DSD or upsampled to the highest rate.
  • An album deleted from the music folders stayed on its artist's page, in the app and the browser.
  • The Android app's album tabs were squashed into a column of letters, and the headings under them said the tabs again.
  • Mandarin Bridge's update said only SecureConnectionError when GitHub's certificate was refused; it now says why (a wrong clock, missing certificates).
