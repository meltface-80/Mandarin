What's new in v1.2.0

Mandarin v1.2.0 and Mandarin Bridge v0.2.1, since v1.1.14.

SQ-tool

Proves your DAC gets exactly the file. Choose a song and a zone, press Record: Mandarin plays it, records what the DAC is fed and compares every sample, with spectrograms and charts. SQ-tool →

High-quality upsampling

×2, ×4 or to the most a device takes, with Standard, Min phase or Gentle filters made as the track plays; PCM made DSD64, DSD128 or DSD256; a highest rate for each device; all gapless. Upsampling and output →

Also new

  • Mandarin Bridge: an Android device, a Linux computer or a Raspberry Pi with a USB DAC is a bit-perfect room, updated from Settings → Updates. More →
  • The signal path on Now playing: every step from the file to the DAC, in plain words. More →
  • Room and headphone correction from an impulse response.
  • DSD to a USB DAC on the server, natively or as DoP; DFF files play as DSD.
  • Album of the Day: one you haven't played in a year or more, all day.
  • This website, with a Downloads page.

Fixed

  • The Android app's USB driver is bit-perfect, and on a FiiO R7 plays the DAC on its port.
  • Streamers such as the FiiO SR11 show the right track, skip none, and no longer stall on taps in a row.
  • New albums appear by themselves moments after they're copied.
  • Away from home, the app keeps its connection after Tailscale restarts.
  • A saved log says what went wrong, hiding only what is yours alone.
Updating: the server from Settings → Updates; the Android app beside it; a Bridge from its own row there (a Bridge from before v0.2.1 needs one last update by hand, from Downloads). Every change is listed in the changelog.
