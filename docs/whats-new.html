What's new in v1.2.0

Mandarin v1.2.0 and Mandarin Bridge v0.2.1, since v1.1.14.

Mandarin Bridge

A USB DAC on an Android device (it was Mandarin USB), a Linux computer or a Raspberry Pi is a room Mandarin plays to, bit-perfect. It is set up from the DAC itself: every rate, depth and DSD rate it takes, nothing to tick, its volume fixed where the DAC has no volume control. Mandarin Bridge →

Bridges updated from Mandarin

Every Bridge has a row on Settings → Updates with its version and its own Check for update: a tap and it updates itself. Their logs are in the app's Save log too.

SQ-tool, in Settings

Proves a zone's DAC gets exactly what is in the file. Choose a song and a zone, press Record: Mandarin plays the song and records what the DAC is fed, and SQ-tool compares every sample, with spectrograms and charts. SQ-tool →

The signal path

Under the album on Now playing: what goes out and how it was made; a tap shows every step from the file to what the DAC is fed, in plain words. The ✓ only where the device itself says the samples reach the DAC untouched. Signal path →

Upsampling and output

A highest rate for each streamer or DAC; Standard, Min phase and Gentle filters; ×16 (705.6 and 768 kHz) plays; PCM made DSD64, DSD128 or DSD256 as it plays, gapless; DSD sent as PCM with its ultrasonic noise taken off above 30 or 50 kHz. Upsampling and output →

Room correction

Load an impulse response (a WAV from REW, Dirac or a headphone measurement), one for each rate or a zip of them, on a device's DSP, and everything it plays runs through it.

DSD to a DAC on the server

Native DSD where the DAC says it takes it, DoP where you tick it, and a DFF file plays as DSD as a DSF does.

A phone with a USB DAC

This phone has a streamer's Output too: upsampled, or PCM made DSD, made by the server and played by the app's USB driver. The driver is bit-perfect: each sample reaches the DAC as it is.

Library and Home

Albums copied into your music folders appear by themselves, moments later. Album of the Day is one you haven't played in a year or more, with its own switch in Settings → Home Screen.

Streamers

A FiiO SR11 is known by its model, shows the right track and its real length, no longer skips a track, and taps on Next, Back, Play and Pause in a row no longer pile up.

The Android app

On a FiiO R7 it plays the DAC on the USB port, not its own. Away from home it keeps a working connection after its Tailscale restarts. A saved log now says what went wrong, hiding only what is yours alone.

And more

32-bit FLAC really is 32 bits (the image's ffmpeg is 7.1). Qobuz and Tidal favourites the service won't give are no longer asked for all day. A search typed while the page starts is kept.

Updating: the server from Settings → Updates; the Android app beside it; a Bridge from its own row there (a Bridge from before v0.2.1 needs one last update by hand, from Downloads). Every change is listed in the changelog.
