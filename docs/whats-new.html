What's new in v1.2.2

Mandarin v1.2.2, since v1.2.1. Mandarin Bridge stays at v0.3.1.

Two acts of one name, two artists

Airbag from Norway and Airbag from Argentina are no longer one artist with both bands' albums. Each album is filed under its act, by the MusicBrainz artist ID in its files (as Picard writes it), else by Mandarin's own MusicBrainz matches; each act has its own tile, page, picture and facts, and a word to tell them apart. Features →

A shorter signal path

Source, Bit depth conversion, Volume levelling where it is on, then what goes out in one line, Gaussian steep ×16 · 7th-order · DSD256; Now playing's line under the title is that same line. Signal path →

Changed

  • The share card is the card alone: its review is always on it, a fifth bigger, with whose words they are. The links under it, If you like this and Settings → Setup → Share Card are gone.
  • Logging is on by default, in the server and the Android app, so a fault seen once is already in the log. Switched off, it stays off. Logs →
  • A track Mandarin stops making on purpose is logged as stopped, with why, not as failed.

Fixed

  • Queuing an album, or Play next, while another played stopped the one playing where Mandarin made it as it played (DSD from PCM, upsampling, an album as one stream).
  • The Android app's Library came back filtered to the last name typed, with no field to clear it, and lost a sort chosen there once an album had been opened over it.
  • The Library Scanner kept saying MusicBrainz isn't answering and stopped ten minutes at a time: it asked MusicBrainz about albums iTunes had matched.
  • An artist's page showed another act of the same name (Airbag from Norway as Spain · since 1998), and the app kept showing it for a while after the server had found the right one.
  • Filing albums under their acts read the whole library again, and wrote the album edits' backup, for every album.
Updating: the server from Settings → Updates; the Android app beside it. Mandarin Bridge has no update this time. The first scan after updating reads every file's tags once more for its artist's MusicBrainz ID. Every change is listed in the changelog.
