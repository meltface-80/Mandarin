Mandarin

Your own music files, played to Sonos rooms, streamers, DACs and your phone

A server on a machine you own scans your music folders. Sonos rooms, the UPnP/DLNA streamers you switch on (a WiiM, a Chord Poly, a FiiO, an AV receiver, a TV), a USB DAC through Mandarin Bridge, and the Mandarin Android app play from it. No subscription or streaming account is needed.
