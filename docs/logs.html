Logs

Logging is in the Android app, the server and the Mandarin Bridges, and is off by default: while it is off, nothing is collected.

Switch it on in the app, Settings → Setup → Logging, when something isn't right. Use the app as you normally would for 24 hours, then tap Save log: the app's log, the server's and each Mandarin Bridge's are saved together to Downloads/Mandarin on the phone, for you to send to the developer. Clear log empties it; switching logging off deletes it, the server's part too.

The log is made without personal data, and the app says so on the same screen. Please look through it before you send it: what you send is your choice.

Captured

What it captures

  • Screens: each screen opened and how long after the tap, what is on screen, where each tap fell, and anything that doesn't flow.
  • Requests: each request the app makes to the server: its address on the server, how long it took and whether it worked. The values in it are left out.
  • The way to the server: home, away (Tailscale) or offline, and networks coming and going.
  • The phone's player: playing or paused, the track's number in the queue, its position and the volume.
  • The app: its memory, crashes and freezes, and Android's own lines for Mandarin.
  • The app's Tailscale: whether it is running, the CPU time it has used, and why it was stopped.
  • The Mandarin Bridges: every Bridge Mandarin knows, under its name, version and build: its last lines (the player, the DAC, a recording, an update), gathered as you save.
  • The server: each line it logs, with what is yours alone taken out.
Left out

What it leaves out

  • Passwords, keys and tokens.
  • Email addresses, account names and logins.
  • Internet and Tailscale addresses.
  • Your own home folder's name in a path.

It keeps what helps find a fault: home-network addresses, the names of rooms and devices, what was playing, error messages.

The app keeps its log in its own storage (two files of 3 MB at most); the server keeps its part in its data folder (two files of 1 MB at most) while logging is on. A Bridge keeps its last lines anyway; they leave it only when you save.
